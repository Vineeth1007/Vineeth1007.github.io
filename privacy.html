Privacy policy

This setup is used only by its owner, to reach the owner's own Google Drive with rclone.

What it uses

It reads and writes files in the owner's own Google Drive, only when the owner runs rclone.

What it keeps

It collects no personal data. Nothing is sold or shared with anyone. The sign-in key is kept only on the owner's own computer and in the owner's own Google Colab sessions.

How to remove access

The owner can remove access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
